Defining Classroom Management

Classroom mangement is...

Classroom management in its most raditional sense is the collection of "actions taken to create a productive, orderly learning environment" (Hoy & Weinstein 2006, p. 181) that can include discipline, socialization, physical environment, and teaching/learning strategies. Classroom management involves a comprehensive system you cna activate immediately.
